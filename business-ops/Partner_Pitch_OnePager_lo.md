## 3. ຂໍ້ສະເໜີສະເພາະສຳລັບ BCEL (ຄູ່ຮ່ວມອັນດັບ 1)

### ເປັນຫຍັງ BCEL ໄດ້ປະໂຫຍດ?
1. **Phalom ເປັນຊ່ອງທາງຂະຫຍາຍຕະຫຼາດ (Sales & Onboarding Channel)** ໄປຫາແມ່ຄ້ານ້ອຍຫຼາຍຮ້ອຍຮ້ານທີ່ພະນັກງານທະນາຄານບໍ່ສາມາດເຂົ້າເຖິງເທື່ອລະຮ້ານໄດ້.
2. **ທຸກລາຍການບັດຕ່າງປະເທດ, Apple/Google Pay, Alipay ແລະ WeChat Pay ແລ່ນຜ່ານລະບົບ BCEL ທັງໝົດ** — ບໍ່ແຍ່ງລູກຄ້າຂອງ BCEL, ແຕ່ເພີ່ມປະລິມານທຸລະກຳໃໝ່ໃຫ້ BCEL.
3. BCEL ມີທັງສອງສ່ວນຢູ່ແລ້ວ: **Online Payment Gateway (CyberSource / 3-D Secure)** + **EzyKip (ຈ່າຍ LAO QR ຈາກບັດຕ່າງປະເທດ)**.

### ສິ່ງດຽວທີ່ Phalom ຂໍຈາກ BCEL

## Executive Summary (English — for Bank / Gateway Leads)

**Phalom** (1st Place, Innovative Business Challenge 2026) is a merchant onboarding and multilingual QR menu platform helping micro-vendors in Laos capture tourist sales they currently lose to cash-only / LAO QR-only friction.

- **What Phalom brings:** Micro-vendor acquisition, Lao-first merchant app + 5-language digital menu, and a ready HMAC-verified webhook architecture. **Phalom never holds customer or merchant funds.**
- **What we seek from BCEL:** API/web-checkout access allowing tourists to scan a Phalom QR with their phone camera (zero app download) and pay via foreign cards (with mandatory 3-D Secure), Apple/Google Pay, Alipay, or WeChat Pay, settling directly into the vendor's LAO QR account, with transparent tourist-paid fees shared between BCEL and Phalom.
