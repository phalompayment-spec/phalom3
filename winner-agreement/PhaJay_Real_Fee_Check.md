# PhaJay's real fee vs. the 1.5% in our pitch

Checked 20 Oct 2026 on PhaJay's own website. The deck, the budget file and the Q&A files were not changed.

## The short answer

- **Where 1.5% came from:** our budget file's source sheet says it. The general fee for taking payments in Laos is 2.5–4% (PayAtlas guide), and we picked a lower round number, 1.5%, as our own estimate. It was never a PhaJay number.
- **PhaJay's real published fee for Lao bank QR is 1.60%.** So our 1.5% was close, a little low.
- **PhaJay's real published fee for Alipay and WeChat Pay is 4.15%.** That is much higher than 1.5%.
- **Our 30% share of PhaJay's fee is still not confirmed by anything.** PhaJay's website doesn't mention any partner, referral or revenue-share program. **This is the real weak point, not the 1.5%.**

## What the tourist pays (shop still gets the full 100,000 kip)

| | Fee | Tourist pays |
|---|---|---|
| Our pitch | 1.5% | 101,500 |
| Real: Lao bank QR | 1.60% | about 101,600 |
| Real: Alipay / WeChat Pay | 4.15% | about 104,300 |
| LOCA Pay (for comparison) | 3% + 5,000 | 108,000 |

**Good news:** even at the real Alipay/WeChat price, the tourist pays less than with LOCA Pay. Slide 7's main point still holds; only the exact number (101,500) is too low for Alipay/WeChat.

"About" because, for the shop to keep exactly 100,000, the fee is taken from the bigger total: 100,000 ÷ (1 − 4.15%) = 104,330.

## Team income and break-even under the real prices

Same plan as the budget file: 9 million kip sales per shop per month; 100 / 250 / 400 / 700 shops; same costs (Q1 20.9M, Q2 23.1M, Q3 36.3M, Q4 44.0M); 40M kip start money.

| Scenario | Team income per shop per month | Income passes costs | Year 1 result | Lowest money left |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pitch:** 1.5% × 30% | 40,500 | Quarter 3 | +9.4M | 23.3M |
| **Real Lao QR**, 30% share | 43,200 | Quarter 3 | +18.3M | 25.2M |
| Real Lao QR, 20% share | 28,800 | Quarter 4 | −29.3M | 7.2M |
| Real Lao QR, 10% share | 14,400 | not in 5 quarters | −76.8M | runs out in Q3 |
| Real Alipay/WeChat, 10% share | 37,350 | Quarter 3 | −1.0M | 21.2M |
| PhaJay shares nothing | 0 | never | −124.3M | runs out in Q2 |
| No share; Phalom adds its own 0.5% | 45,000 | Quarter 2 | +24.2M | 25.9M |

What this means:

1. **If PhaJay agrees to share 30%, the real price makes our numbers slightly better, not worse** (43,200 instead of 40,500 per shop).
2. **The plan works only if Phalom earns about 38,000 kip per shop per month** (year-1 costs 124.3M ÷ 3,300 shop-months). Where that money comes from is the question PhaJay has to answer.
3. **If PhaJay shares less than about 26% of its Lao QR fee, or nothing, we need our own small fee.** For example, 0.5% on top (500 kip on a 100,000 sale). With Lao QR the tourist would then pay about 102,100, still far below LOCA Pay.
4. Our 9M kip sales per shop and the number of shops are still our own estimates, and they move the result as much as the fee does.

## What to say to the organizers if asked

Plain and true, in one breath:

> "The 1.5% in our pitch was our own estimate. PhaJay's published price is 1.60% for Lao bank QR and 4.15% for Alipay and WeChat Pay. Our share of PhaJay's fee is not agreed yet. Meeting PhaJay is our first step, and we will report the real terms. If they don't share their fee, Phalom adds its own small fee, and the tourist still pays less than with LOCA Pay."

Lao version:

> "ຄ່າທຳນຽມ 1.5% ໃນການນຳສະເໜີ ແມ່ນການຄາດຄະເນຂອງທີມ. ລາຄາທີ່ PhaJay ປະກາດໄວ້ ແມ່ນ 1.60% ສຳລັບ QR ທະນາຄານລາວ ແລະ 4.15% ສຳລັບ Alipay ແລະ WeChat Pay. ສ່ວນແບ່ງຂອງທີມ ຍັງບໍ່ໄດ້ຕົກລົງກັບ PhaJay. ການພົບ PhaJay ແມ່ນບາດກ້າວທຳອິດຂອງພວກເຮົາ ແລະ ຈະລາຍງານຕົວເລກຈິງ. ຖ້າ PhaJay ບໍ່ແບ່ງ, Phalom ຈະເກັບຄ່າບໍລິການນ້ອຍໆຂອງຕົນເອງ ແລະ ນັກທ່ອງທ່ຽວກໍ່ຍັງຈ່າຍໜ້ອຍກວ່າ LOCA Pay."

Why this is safe: the pitch already said the source was a "team calculation", slide 16 asked for an introduction to PhaJay (not a signed deal), and the competition judged the idea. Saying the real numbers now, before signing, shows the team checks its facts.

**In the agreement:** don't accept a target that depends on PhaJay's revenue share or on reaching a set income. Targets we control, like "meet PhaJay" and "start a pilot with up to 100 shops", are safer (see Phalom_Agreement_Prep.md, section 4, row 5).

## Questions to ask PhaJay

1. Do you have a partner or referral program for apps that bring you merchants? What share?
2. Can a shop add your fee to the customer's total, or must the shop pay it?
3. Can Thai tourists pay your QR with a Thai bank app (PromptPay / Thai QR)?
4. Is there a lower rate for many small shops signed up through one partner?
5. How long until the money reaches the shop's bank account?
