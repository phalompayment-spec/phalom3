Phalom × PhaJay: the core end-to-end business processes

Research of 23 Oct 2026, from PhaJay's public site and developer docs, Bank of the Lao PDR rules, and the competition. Nothing here is agreed with PhaJay. Confirmed = written by the source. Unknown = ask. Estimate = our own guess.

Bottom line

1. The pitch's core promise is already free elsewhere

Thai (since mid-2024), Vietnamese (since about early 2025) and Chinese UnionPay (since Dec 2024) tourists can already pay any shop's normal LAO QR with their own apps. Western tourists can use LOCA Pay or EzyKip. Thailand + China + Vietnam = 3.77M of 4.58M visitors in 2025.

2. A PhaJay QR may be worse for tourists

PhaJay's docs: the BCEL QR it makes "is not a Lao QR, so it can only be scanned and paid using the BCEL One app." A Thai or Chinese tourist's app probably can't pay it. Alipay/WeChat Pay are on PhaJay's price list (4.15%) but have no API docs and no sandbox.

3. But PhaJay has a ready-made way for us to earn

Split Payment: a parent account (Phalom) with sub-accounts (shops). PhaJay splits each payment automatically, and the parent keeps a set % (above 0, up to 90%). No revenue-share deal needed, and Phalom never holds the shop's money.

4. Hard blockers for street vendors

PhaJay's KYC asks for business name/registration, business documents and 5 real paid transactions first. Payouts are manual (claim, then an admin transfers), with no stated timing. Refunds are full only. Unclear whether tiny shops with no business licence can become sub-accounts.

What this means: "Tourists pay with their own app" can't be the whole business, because banks and LAPNet already give shops that for free. Phalom's value has to be what banks don't do: tourists finding the shop (map, recommendations, menus in 5 languages), plain-Lao tools, and the shop knowing who its buyers are. PhaJay is worth it only for the payments that LAO QR doesn't cover (Alipay/WeChat, cards) and as a way to collect Phalom's fee, if the sub-account terms work for small shops.

Rules (Bank of the Lao PDR)

  • Decision 511/BOL (19 Jun 2025) on payment services replaced Decision 1058 (2016). Cross-border services (accepting foreign payment instruments, cross-border transfers) need BOL authorisation and at least 1 billion kip capital, and the firm must first hold a domestic payment licence. Secondary source
  • The Payment System Law was amended (unofficial English, Dec 2025). We found nothing on "technical service providers" or platforms that never touch money.
  • So: if Phalom ever receives tourists' money and pays shops out, it is very likely doing a licensed payment service. Model A (PhaJay splits directly into each shop's sub-account) and models B/C (shop's own bank QR) keep Phalom out of the money flow. Confirm with BOL or a lawyer (open decision 5 in BUSINESS.md).

Questions only PhaJay can answer (ask before choosing)

  1. Tourists: Is a PhaJay QR (JDB, LDB, IB, STB, APB, M-Money) a real LAO QR that Thai bank apps, VietQR and UnionPay can pay? Your docs say the BCEL QR is BCEL One only.
  2. Alipay / WeChat Pay: How do we turn them on, is there an API and a sandbox, and does the shop receive kip?
  3. Sub-accounts: How does Phalom get agent (parent) status? Can we create shop sub-accounts by API?
  4. Small shops: Can a vendor with only an ID card and a bank account be a sub-account, without a business licence or 5 real payments?
  5. Payouts: How many days from Claim to the shop's bank? Withdrawal fee? Can payouts be automatic? Can the parent claim for its sub-accounts?
  6. Fees: Can the shop add the fee to the tourist's price? Is there a lower rate for a platform with many small shops?

What to do next

  1. Send PhaJay the questions above (Lao draft added to HUMAN_TODO.md). Their answer to Q1 decides whether model A is worth building.
  2. Visit 5–10 real vendors in the pilot market. Ask: Do you already have a bank QR? Do Thai or Chinese tourists pay it? Would you pay X kip a month for a menu in 5 languages and a spot on a tourist map? Write the answers down. This is the evidence the business model is missing.
  3. Ask BCEL what OnePay Shop / LAO QR costs a merchant (no public rate found).
  4. Don't build the PhaJay integration yet. Start the pilot on model D: shop keeps its bank QR, and Phalom adds menu, poster, map and stats.
