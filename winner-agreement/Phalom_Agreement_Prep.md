## 2. Is the prize money our project budget?

**Maybe. We can't tell yet.** [Guess]

- "Prize money" alone usually means the team can use it freely.
- But a signed agreement with a week to review usually means there are **conditions**: use it for the project, report on progress, maybe pay it in parts.
- Our pitch asked for **40 million kip** (20.9M for the first 3 months, 19.1M kept aside until income passes costs in quarter 3). If the prize is a different amount:
  - **Less than 40M:** show a smaller plan (fewer new shops in quarter 1, less advertising) and say where the rest would come from.
  - **About 40M or more:** our budget file already explains every kip: `pitch-redesign/Phalom_Budget.xlsx`.

**Ask the organizer directly:** "Is the prize money a free prize, or a grant that must be spent on the Phalom project?"

## 4. What we expect the agreement to say

All [Guess]. Usual sections, and what to check in each.

| Section | What it probably says | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Parties | Organizer (SPK) and team Phalom, with each member named | Every member's name is spelled right. Who signs for the team. |
| 2. Prize | Amount, in kip, paid on 15 Nov by bank transfer or cheque | The exact amount. Is it **one payment or in parts**? Is **tax** taken off, and who pays it? |
| 3. Use of the money | Must be used for the Phalom project; maybe our budget is attached | Is it free prize money or a grant? If a grant, the budget lines should match ours, with room to move money between lines. |
| 4. Team duties | Carry out the project, send reports (monthly or quarterly), keep receipts | Reports should be short and not too frequent. Targets must be things we control. |
| 5. Milestones | Maybe targets such as number of shops or a pilot by a date | **Don't promise** a signed PhaJay contract or 700 shops: those depend on others and are estimates. Better: "start a pilot with up to 100 shops" and "meet PhaJay". |
| 6. Paying back | If money is misused or the project stops, some or all may have to be returned | Paying back should only apply to **misuse**, not to missing a target in good faith. |
| 7. Ownership of the idea | Who owns the app, the name Phalom and the pitch | **The team should keep ownership.** The organizer may use our name and pitch for promotion only. Watch for any clause giving them a **share of the company** or first right to invest. |
| 8. Publicity | Organizer can use photos, names, logo; we mention them in our materials | Fine in general. Ask to approve anything said about our numbers or partners. |
| 9. Confidentiality | Both sides keep private information private | Should go both ways. |
| 10. Team changes | What happens if a member leaves | A member leaving should not cancel the whole agreement. |
| 11. Ending the agreement | When either side can end it | Clear reasons, written notice. |
| 12. Law and disputes | Lao law; talk first, then a named office or court | Normal. |
| 13. Language and signatures | Lao (maybe also English); signed by both sides | If two languages, which one counts. |
