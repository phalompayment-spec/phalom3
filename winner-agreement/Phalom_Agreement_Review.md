# Winner agreement: review of the organizer's draft

Draft received 25 Oct 2026 (PDF, 4 pages, Lao, from Superketz / CATZ / ISF). The PDF itself is **not** in this repo (public repo); keep it in the team's private files. This is a plain reading, not legal advice. Compared with the guesses in `Phalom_Agreement_Prep.md`.

## Short version

- **Good news:** much lighter than we feared. No conditions on how we spend the prize, no progress reports, no shop targets, no paying back, no share of the company. The team keeps ownership of Phalom (4.3). Confidentiality goes both ways (5).
- **What it costs us is time:** 12 months as Brand Ambassador (posts, events), a committee/mentor role in IBC 2027, and two videos. All of this counts as "paid for by the prize" (8.4).
- **Biggest gaps:** the **prize amount is not written anywhere**, there is **no end date** (10.1), video deadlines are blank, and the ambassador duties have no limit.

## Clause by clause

| Clause | What it says | Our view |
|---|---|---|
| Parties | Party 1: Superketz / CATZ / ISF, one representative, business number blank. Party 2: team name, one representative, prize, ID card, address. | Ask which organization actually signs and pays. Only one person signs for us and gives their ID and address, so that person is personally bound. Ask to attach a list of all members, and that a member leaving doesn't cancel it. |
| 1 Purpose | Duties of the winning team and cooperation after the competition. | Fine. |
| 2.1 Future activities | Join Superketz/ISF activities **when invited and both sides agree**. | Fine. |
| 2.2 Share ISF news | Help promote ISF through suitable channels. | Fine, but no limit. |
| 2.3 Their logos | Use their data and logos only as allowed. | Fine. |
| 2.4 Brand Ambassador | **12 months "or as set"** from signing: PR, events, posts on our own social media, as agreed. | Remove "or as set". Ask for a clear amount (e.g. posts per month, events per year), notice in advance, and the right to say no during exams. Confirm there is no exclusivity (we can still work with banks or other sponsors). |
| 2.5 IBC 2027 committee | We **agree to** be committee/advisor/co-organizer: advise, mentor, help screen entries. | Unlike 2.1, this is a firm duty. Ask for the same "when invited and agreed" wording, an estimate of days, and travel/food covered on event days. |
| 3.1–3.3 Sharing experience | Talks, mentoring, interviews, online content, by prior agreement. | Fine. |
| 3.4 Two videos | Post-evaluation video, and a video inviting people to IBC 2027. **Deadlines left blank.** | Ask for dates, length, quality (is a phone video OK?), and who pays for production. |
| 4.1 Ownership of project work | Ownership of work made "under the project" must be set in that project's agreement. | Vague. Ask to state plainly that Phalom's name, idea, app, pitch and business plan belong to the team. |
| 4.2–4.3 Existing work | Earlier work stays separate; signing does **not** transfer our work to them. | Good. This is the key protection. |
| 4.4 Video rights | Videos from 3.4 belong to **CATZ alone**. We let CATZ/Superketz/ISF use our names, photos, voices and videos in **all channels, with no fee**, no time limit. | Acceptable for competition promotion. Ask to add: Phalom's name/logo/app shown in the videos stays ours; no use of members' faces in ads for unrelated products without asking; the team checks any Phalom numbers or partner names before they're published (many numbers are estimates and no partner is signed). |
| 5 Confidentiality | Both sides keep each other's business information private; public info excluded. | Good, mutual. Note: our pitch material is already public in this repo. |
| 6 Publicity | They may use results, photos, videos "within agreed consent"; partner names/logos need permission. | Fine. 6.2 also binds us: we can't name BCEL, PhaJay etc. as partners without their permission. |
| 7 Projects and KPIs | Scope, KPIs and reports **only for projects both sides agree on**. | Good: no KPIs from this agreement itself. |
| 8 Money | Revenue, fees or sponsorship in any joint project must be agreed in writing first (8.1–8.2). We pay no unagreed costs (8.3). Ambassador, committee and videos are covered by the prize (8.4). | 8.4 refers to a prize amount that isn't in the agreement. Ask to add: amount, payment date (15 Nov per notice), method, tax. |
| 9 Disputes | Talk first, then applicable law. | Normal. |
| 10 Term | From signing **until an end date both sides agree** (no date given). Changes in writing. Duties already incurred, confidentiality and rights survive. | Ask for a real end date, e.g. the end of IBC 2027 or 12 months after signing. |
| 11 General | Changes in writing, both sides confirm. | Fine. |

Typos to point out: heading 3 "ການແບງປັນ" → "ການແບ່ງປັນ"; signature block "ທິມຜູ້ຊະນະ" → "ທີມຜູ້ຊະນະ".

Not in the draft (from our prep list): prize amount, payment schedule, tax, use of money, reports, milestones, repayment, equity/first right to invest, team changes, language. Missing repayment/equity/milestones is good for us; missing prize details is not.
